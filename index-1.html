About My Research

My research focuses on exploring deep learning techniques to solve challenging problems in medical image analysis.

I am interested in developing and evaluating AI models that can learn meaningful representations from medical images. My work explores feature extraction, multimodal learning, data augmentation, segmentation, and efficient architectures for medical image understanding.

RESEARCH 01

Brain Tumor Classification Using MRI

Ongoing Research

Exploring a hybrid framework for classifying brain MRI images into glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor, and no-tumor categories. The approach investigates targeted quantum-inspired data augmentation and advanced feature fusion.

Medical AI MRI QGAN DINOv2 LoRA Attention

Research focus: Improving feature representations and distinguishing visually similar tumor categories.

RESEARCH 02

Brain Tumor Segmentation on BraTS2020

Ongoing Research

Investigating 3D brain tumor segmentation using multimodal MRI sequences. The proposed framework combines modality-aware hypergraph learning with state-space-based feature modeling to capture spatial and cross-modal relationships.

BraTS2020 3D U-Net Hypergraph State Space Models Segmentation

Research focus: Segmenting whole tumor, tumor core, and enhancing tumor regions while modeling long-range 3D context.
